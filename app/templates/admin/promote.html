What happens

Every active student moves up one class: Nursery to LKG, LKG to UKG, UKG to Class 1, and so on. Class 12 students are marked passed out; they can still log in to see old results.

Students moving into Class 11 have no stream yet. Set it on their profile afterwards. Exams, results and fees from earlier years are not changed.
